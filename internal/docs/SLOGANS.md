# The AIVIA slogan book

Coined in the Sunny + Claude sessions; provenance noted. Some are
LAW (they bind design), some are DOCTRINE (they explain design),
some are COPY (candidates for listing/demo — final wording is
Sunny's). Add new ones the moment they're coined; the record is the
point.

## The founding law

- **"Operations are the product."** — Sunny, 2026-08-13. Plan,
  confirm, execute, display; the answer is a caption. (LAW; in
  METHODOLOGY.)
- **"Translated by LLM, regulated by human."** — 2026-08-13. (LAW)
- **"Intentions decay; only enforcement survives."** — enforcement
  lineage, 2026-08-21. (LAW)
- **"Proof where proof exists; witness where it doesn't."** — the
  asymmetry, METHODOLOGY. (LAW)
