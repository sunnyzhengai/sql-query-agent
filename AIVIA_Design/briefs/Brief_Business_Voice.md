Proposed sentence (gate-PASSED, every content word source-traced):

> The base population's ED encounters that had an 'ED BOARDER PATIENTS' event — one row per encounter, carrying only its encounter id; a membership list.

Stored sentence today, for contrast: "One record per distinct encounter id, matched in ed event info records: A matching record exists in a nested selection reading the base pop selection defined earlier in this procedure; The event type is one of the values '2600000007' (noted 'ED BOARDER PATIENTS'); carrying the encounter id."
