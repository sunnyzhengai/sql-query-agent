==== COOK_RPT_USP_CCHCS_ADT_MONTHLY_INPATIENT_CENSUS_TOTALS_SSRS ====
basis 07.2.0

[gate_passed] file: One row is: one inpatient census event for one patient at one bed or room location, representing that patient’s census status at a specific effective date and time.

Who's in it: patients with a recorded Census event during the chosen reporting period, linked to a patient, in the chosen service area and chosen location. Choosing All for service area includes every service area, and choosing All for location includes every location.

Each row shows: where the patient was assigned, how the encounter was classified, the patient’s service, which service area and department the event belongs to, and identifiers and timing details for the census event.

Time window: the report includes census events with an effective date from the chosen start date through the chosen end date, including the full end date. It shows the data as it stood at the end of the chosen as-of date: only events entered by then are included. Events marked Canceled are still included if they were not deleted until after the as-of date, so the report reflects what users would have seen on that date. It also includes the day of month taken from the event’s effective date as the census day.

Excludes: records not linked to a patient; departments CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection contains census ADT events for patients with a recorded patient identifier, during the chosen event date range, using the data as it existed on the As Of date. It keeps events that were created before the end of the As Of date and excludes events canceled before that point, while still keeping events that were canceled later so the report reflects the historical state as of that date.

For each included census event, it brings back the patient, event date and census day, patient class, patient service, department, room, bed, service area, and the related department, room, bed, and ADT event identifiers. It excludes records for these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR, and also excludes records not linked to a patient or not matching the other required linked selections.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the report’s service area filter list. It includes one row for each department’s service area, and it also includes the special choice **All**, represented here by the value **0**.

In business terms, this lets the person running the report choose one or more service areas to limit the report, or choose **All** to include every service area. The list is built from hospital location and ADT reference information tied to departments, rooms, beds, service areas, patient class, and patient service reference data.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the list of revenue locations chosen when running the report. It includes either the specific revenue location linked to a department, or All when the choice is 0.

The location list is built from hospital event history together with bed, room, department, service area, and patient class information. Each row simply represents one chosen revenue location value for use in the report.
[gate_passed] field: Shows the event type for census events only, during the chosen effective-date period including the end date, and as the data stood on the As Of date including that date. It includes canceled census events only when they were still not deleted as of that date, lets the person running the report choose Service Area and Location with All available for each, and excludes records without a patient plus activity from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: Shows the subtype of each Census event for patients who had an effective event during the chosen start and end dates, using the record as it stood on the chosen as-of date. The report includes only patients with a recorded patient identifier, respects the chosen Service Area and Location selections with All available for each, excludes the listed departments, and keeps canceled events only when they were still not deleted as of that as-of date.
[gate_passed] field: The effective time is the date and time when the census event took effect for the patient. It only appears for census events with a recorded patient, in the chosen date range including the full end date, using the data as it stood at the end of the chosen as-of date; Service Area and Location can be chosen when running the report, with All including every value, and it excludes the listed departments plus canceled events unless they were still not deleted by the end of the as-of date.
[gate_passed] field: The patient ID is shown for patients who had a Census event in the reporting period, using events effective from the chosen start date through the chosen end date, including the full end date, and only counting events that existed as of the chosen as-of date. Canceled Census events are left out unless they were still not deleted by that as-of date, and the results are limited to the service areas and locations chosen when running the report, where choosing All includes every service area or location.

This excludes records without a patient ID and events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The abbreviated patient class shown here comes from census events during the chosen date range, based on events effective from the start date through the end date inclusive, and it reflects the data as it stood at the as-of date. Service area and location are chosen when running the report, and choosing All includes every service area or location.

Excluded are events from the listed departments CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR, along with records without a patient, and canceled events unless they were still not deleted as of the as-of date.
[gate_passed] field: **Department**: the department abbreviation for each qualifying census event.

**Time window / Includes / Excludes**: includes census events with an effective time during the chosen start and end dates, treating the end date as inclusive, and shows the data as it stood at the chosen as-of date; canceled events are excluded unless they were still not deleted by the day after that as-of date. It only includes records with a patient ID, only for the service areas and locations chosen when running the report, where choosing **All** includes every service area or location, and it excludes these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The room field shows the room name from a patient census event for a patient with a recorded patient identifier. It only includes events in the chosen Start Date through End Date, using an inclusive End Date, as the data stood at the chosen As Of Date, and only in the service areas and locations chosen when running the report, where choosing All includes every service area or location.

Excluded: canceled census events unless they were still present as of the As Of Date, records without a patient link, and events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: **The bed label**  
Shows the bed assigned on a qualifying census event for a patient stay. It is limited to patients with a recorded patient identifier, in the service areas and locations chosen when running the report, with **All** available for either choice, during the effective date range including both the start and end dates, as the data stood through the As Of date including that date; canceled census events are excluded unless they were still not deleted by the day after the As Of date, and several named procedural and emergency departments are excluded.
[gate_passed] field: Shows the patient’s service from census events during the chosen effective-date range, as the data stood at the chosen as-of date; the end dates are inclusive, and canceled census events are included only if they were still not deleted by that as-of date. The report is limited to patients with an ID, to the service areas and locations chosen when running the report where choosing All includes every value, and it excludes these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: Shows when the census event was deleted, for census activity during the chosen date range, using an inclusive end date, and only as it stood by the chosen as-of date. If a census event was canceled, it is included only when its deletion happened after the as-of date, so cancellations already deleted by that point are not shown.

Excludes records without a patient, records outside the chosen service area or location unless All was chosen, and records from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The event time is the date and time when the Census event was recorded for the patient. It is shown only for patients with an identified patient record, for events effective during the chosen date range using an inclusive end date, entered by the as-of date, limited to the chosen Service Area and Location or All, excluding the listed departments, and it reflects data as it stood on the as-of date, including canceled events only if they were still not deleted by then.
[gate_passed] field: Service area is the name of the service area tied to each census event that is in scope. The report includes census activity with effective dates during the chosen start and end dates, uses the data as it stood at the as-of date, lets the person running the report choose one or more service areas and revenue locations or choose All, and excludes records without a patient and activity from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: Department ID for census events that were active in the reporting period from the chosen start date through the chosen end date, using data as it stood at the end of the chosen as-of date. The Service Area and Location choices are selected when running the report, and choosing All includes every service area or location.

Excludes canceled census events unless they were still present as of the as-of date, records without a patient, and these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The room ID is the room tied to a patient census event during the chosen date range, based on events effective from the start date through the end date inclusive, and shown as the data stood on the as-of date. It is limited to records with a patient ID, in the service areas and locations chosen when running the report, where choosing All includes every service area or location.

Excludes canceled census events unless they were still present as of the as-of date, and excludes the departments CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: This is the identifier of the bed tied to a patient census event, so it shows which bed the patient was assigned to for that census record.

Time window: it includes census activity with an effective date during the chosen start and end dates, treating the end date as inclusive, and it shows the data as it stood by the chosen as-of date; canceled census events are left out unless they were still not deleted by that as-of date. Excludes: records without a patient, records outside the chosen service area or location selections, and records from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR; when running the report, choosing All for service area or location includes every value.
[gate_passed] field: The event id is the unique identifier for a census event that was in scope during the chosen effective date range, using an inclusive end date, and as the data stood on the As Of date. It only includes events tied to a patient, chosen when running the report for the selected service area and location, with All available for either choice, and it excludes the listed departments plus canceled events unless they were still present as of the As Of date.
[gate_passed] field: The field shows the calendar day of the census event’s effective time. It includes census events effective during the chosen start and end dates, treats the end date as inclusive, reflects the data as it stood by the chosen as-of date, keeps canceled events only when they were still present as of that date, and is limited to patients with a recorded patient id in the chosen service areas and locations, where choosing All means every service area or location; it excludes the departments CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
