==== COOK_RPT_USP_CCHCS_ADT_MONTHLY_INPATIENT_CENSUS_TOTALS_SSRS ====
basis 07.2.0

[gate_passed] file: One row is: one inpatient census event for one patient at a specific effective date and time, representing where the patient was counted in the hospital census.

Who's in it: patients with a recorded patient identifier who had a Census event during the chosen date range, in departments tied to the chosen service area and chosen location filters. Choosing All for service area includes every service area, and choosing All for location includes every location. The report leaves out events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.

Each row shows: the census event details, the patient’s hospital classification and service, the care location where the patient was counted, service area and department identifiers, and the census day within the month.

Time window: it includes census events whose effective date and time fall from the chosen start date through the chosen end date, including the whole end date. It shows the data as it stood at the end of the chosen as-of date: the event had to be created by then, and a canceled event is still included if it was not deleted until after that date.

Excludes: records without a patient linked; canceled census events that had already been deleted by the end of the chosen as-of date; events from the listed emergency, procedural, operating room, and psychiatry departments.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection contains census ADT events for patients, tied to the unit, room, bed, patient class, patient service, and service area in effect for the event. It includes events scheduled to happen from the chosen start date through the chosen end date, treats the end date as inclusive, and shows the data as it stood at the chosen as-of date by keeping records created before the day after that date and keeping canceled records only when they were not deleted until that point or later. It excludes records without a patient, records that do not match the other required selections, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection keeps departments whose service area was chosen when running the report. If the person running the report chose All, it keeps every service area; otherwise it keeps only departments in the listed service areas.

It uses department, room, bed, ADT event history, service area, patient class, and patient service reference information to support that service-area filtering. The output from this step is simply a qualifying service-area match for a department, marked with a constant value of 1.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the list of location choices chosen when running the report. It keeps a location when the choice is **All** using the value **0**, or when the chosen value matches the revenue location linked to the department on the ADT event.

The location list is built from ADT event history and the related bed, room, department, service area, patient class, and patient service reference information. Each returned choice carries a constant value of **1**.
[gate_passed] field: Identifies ADT census events for patients during the chosen date range, using the event date through the end of the end date, and reflects the data as it stood on the As Of date: events must already have been created by then, and canceled events are still included if they were not deleted until after that As Of date. Excludes records without a patient, records that do not match the separate required patient selections, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.

This field returns the event type, which in this extract is Census.
[gate_passed] field: Shows the census event’s status for each included bed movement record, such as whether it remained active or was later canceled.

Time window and scope: this comes from census events that were scheduled during the chosen date range, counted through the end of the chosen As Of date, so a canceled event is still included if it was not deleted until after that date; excludes records without a patient, records not matching the report’s other required linked selections, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The field is the date and time when the census ADT event was scheduled to occur. It only includes census events during the chosen reporting dates, using an inclusive end date, as they existed as of the chosen As Of date; canceled events are still included if they were not deleted until after that As Of date, and the extract excludes records without a patient, records not linked to the required related selections, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The patient identifier for each census event that was scheduled to happen during the chosen date range, using data as it existed on the As Of date. Excludes canceled events that were already removed before that As Of date, records without a patient, records not in the required linked selections, and events in these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The patient class shown is the hospital encounter class tied to each census ADT event, for patients with a recorded patient identifier whose event fell during the chosen date range, as the data stood on the As Of date; the end date is treated inclusively, and choosing this event set excludes activity in these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.  
It is based on census events only, and excludes records that were canceled before the As Of date, records not linked to a patient, records outside the two required linked record sets, and the excluded departments listed above.
[gate_passed] field: Department abbreviation for each census ADT event in the chosen date range, using the event’s effective unit at the time it occurred. Time window: includes events from Start Date through End Date inclusive, counted as they stood as of the As Of Date, so events created after that date are left out and canceled events are still included only if they were canceled after that date; Excludes: events without a patient, events not in the separately defined required patient selections, and events from these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The patient room from the census ADT event, based on where the patient was assigned at the event’s effective time.

Includes census events that occurred during the chosen date range, using an inclusive end date, as the data stood on the chosen As Of date; excludes canceled events unless they were still active as of that date, records without a patient, records not linked to the required related records, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The bed name for each census event, based on the bed linked to that event at the time it took effect.

Time window and excludes: includes census events that were supposed to happen during the chosen start and end dates, with the end date treated as inclusive, and shows the data as it stood on the chosen as-of date, so canceled events are still included if they were not deleted until after that date. Excludes events without a patient, events not found in the two required related selections, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The patient service is the hospital service tied to the patient’s census event, such as the clinical service managing the stay. It is shown for census events that happened during the chosen date range, as the data stood on the As Of date, excluding canceled events removed before that date, records without a patient, records not in the required linked selections, and events in these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: When the report is run, this field shows when the census event record was deleted in the system. It is populated for census events during the chosen date range, using the data as it stood on the As Of date, and it excludes events from these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: When the report is run, this field shows when the census ADT event record was entered into the system. It only appears for census events tied to a patient and the required linked records, during the chosen event date range, as the data stood on the As Of date, excluding listed procedural and emergency-style units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The service area is the service area tied to the department on the census event, shown as the service area name.

It comes from census ADT events for patients with an event time during the chosen date range, using the data as it stood as of the chosen As Of date; this excludes canceled events that were already canceled by that date, events created after that date, records without a patient, records not in the separately defined required populations, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The field is the unique identifier of the department or unit tied to each census ADT event, based on the unit recorded at the event’s effective time. It is taken for events during the chosen date range, using the data as it stood on the As Of date, and excludes canceled events that were already canceled by then, events not linked to a patient, records failing the report’s other required patient and encounter links, and events from these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The room identifier for the census event’s assigned room, taken from the room attached to that event.

Time window and Excludes are applied at the event level before this is returned: it reflects census events that happened during the chosen date range, using the data as it stood on the As Of date, and excludes canceled events unless they were still not canceled by that As Of date, records without a patient, events outside the separately defined patient selections, and events in these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: Bed record ID for each census ADT event in the chosen event date range, using the data as it stood on the As Of date; the end dates are inclusive, so events on the End Date are included, and events canceled before the As Of date are excluded. Excludes records without a patient, records not in the two required linked selections, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The delivered field is the unique identifier of each census ADT event for patients with a recorded patient ID and matching records in two other required report selections. It includes events that were scheduled to happen during the chosen date range, reflects the data as it existed at the chosen As Of date, keeps canceled events only if they were not deleted until after that As Of date, and excludes events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The patient movement event happened during the chosen reporting dates, using an inclusive end date, and it is counted as the data stood on the As Of date: it includes census events that were created before the day after that As Of date, keeps canceled events only if they were not deleted until that day or later, and excludes events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.  
This field is the calendar day of that event’s effective time, with only events linked to a patient and to the required related records included.
