==== COOK_RPT_USP_CCHCS_ADT_MONTHLY_INPATIENT_CENSUS_TOTALS_SSRS ====
basis 07.2.0

[gate_passed] file: One row is: one census ADT event for a patient, representing that patient’s recorded inpatient location status at a point in time.

Who's in it: patients with a recorded patient identifier who had Census ADT events during the chosen date range, in included departments and service areas, and not in these excluded units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR. Service area and location are chosen when running the report, and choosing 0 means All.

Each row shows: the census event status, when it was supposed to occur, who the patient is, where the patient was assigned organizationally and physically, and key encounter location classifications such as patient class and service.

Time window: it includes events whose event time falls from the chosen start date through the chosen end date, with the end date treated inclusively. It shows the data as it stood at the end of the chosen as-of date: the event had to exist by then, and events canceled before that point are left out. If an event was later canceled after the as-of date, it is still included because it was still part of the record as of that date.

Excludes: records without a patient identifier, records outside the chosen service area or location choices, records in the specifically excluded units listed above, records canceled on or before the as-of date, and records that do not have matches in the two separately defined required selection lists.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection contains inpatient census ADT events for patients with a recorded patient ID, during the chosen event date range, using the event’s effective department, room, bed, patient class, patient service, and service area details. It shows the data as it existed on the chosen As Of date, so events must have been created before the day after that date, and canceled events are still included if they were not deleted until that date had passed; the end dates are inclusive. It excludes records not linked to the required related selections and excludes events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection keeps departments whose service area was chosen when running the report. If the choice includes All, it keeps every service area; otherwise it keeps only departments in the listed service areas.

It uses hospital event history together with department, room, bed, service area, and patient class reference information, and it also brings in patient service when available. Each row in this selection simply marks an eligible service area choice for a department.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the list of location choices chosen when running the report. It includes either **All** when the choice is **0**, or the revenue location linked to the department on the ADT event.

The location comes from the department tied to the event, using the related room, bed, and service area details available for that hospital movement history. It carries a constant flag of **1** for each selected item.
