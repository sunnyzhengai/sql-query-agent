==== COOK_RPT_USP_CCHCS_ADT_MONTHLY_INPATIENT_CENSUS_TOTALS_SSRS ====
basis 07.2.0

[gate_passed] file: One row is: one inpatient census event for a patient placement on a specific census date, as recorded in ADT history

Who's in it: patients with a linked census event during the chosen date range, in the chosen service areas and chosen locations; choosing All for service area includes every service area, and choosing All for location includes every location

Each row shows: the patient’s census placement, where the patient was assigned, how the encounter was classified, the organizational area, and the event identifiers and timing details

Time window: it includes census events whose effective date falls from the start date through the end date, counting the end date in full. It shows the data as it stood at the end of the as-of date: the event itself had to exist by then, and canceled census events are still included if they were not deleted until after the as-of date

Excludes: records not linked to a patient; departments CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR
[gate_passed] scope: This selection contains census ADT events for patients, limited to events that were scheduled to happen during the chosen date range, using an inclusive end date, and showing the data as it stood on the chosen As Of date. It keeps events that were not canceled, and it also keeps canceled events when the cancellation happened after the As Of date, so the report still reflects what was on file at that point in time.

Each row carries the patient, event time, census day, patient class, patient service, department, room, bed, service area, and the related department, room, bed, and ADT event identifiers. It excludes records without a patient, records that do not match the other required report selections, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the list of service areas chosen when running the report. It includes either the specific service areas tied to departments, or All when the choice is 0.

It is built from ADT event history together with the department, room, bed, and service area reference files, plus the lookup used to describe hospital patient class. It carries a constant flag of 1 for each selected service area entry.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the list of revenue locations chosen when running the report. It includes every revenue location linked to a department whose identifier was chosen, and if the choice includes All, then every linked revenue location is included.

Each row represents one selected revenue location value, with a constant marker showing that it was included in the selection. The location list is built from hospital event, bed, room, department, service area, patient class, and patient service reference data so the report can limit results to the chosen revenue locations.
[gate_passed] field: The field shows the ADT event category for census records only, so its value here is Census. It is based on events that occurred during the chosen date range, as the data stood at the end of the chosen As Of date, excluding records without a patient, records not in the required linked populations, and events in these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: Shows whether the census event was active or later canceled for each patient movement record included in the report.

Time window and excludes: this comes from census events that were scheduled during the chosen start through end dates, using the data as it stood at the chosen As Of date; canceled events are still included only when they were not deleted until after that As Of date, and the report excludes records without a patient, records not in the two required linked selections, and events tied to these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The field is the date and time when the census event was effective. It includes census events during the chosen reporting period, treats the end date as inclusive, reflects the event record as it stood on the chosen As Of date, excludes listed procedural and emergency units, and excludes records without a patient or without the required linked records from the other report selections.
[gate_passed] field: The patient identifier for each qualifying census movement event in the chosen date range. Time window: events are included when they happened from the Start Date through the End Date, using an inclusive End Date, and the result shows the data as it stood on the As Of Date, so canceled events are still included if they were not deleted until after that date; excludes events without a patient, events not in the required linked patient selections, and events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The patient class shown is the hospital encounter class tied to each census ADT event, such as inpatient or observation, for patients with a recorded patient identifier and matching required linked records.  
It is taken from census events scheduled during the chosen date range, using data as it stood at the As Of date, excluding listed procedural and emergency units, excluding records not linked to a patient, and keeping canceled events only if they were still not canceled as of that As Of date.
[gate_passed] field: Department abbreviation for each census ADT event, using the unit in effect at the event time. Time window: includes events that happened during the chosen Start Date through End Date, counts data as it stood on the As Of Date, and keeps canceled events only if they were still present on that As Of Date; Excludes: events without a patient, events not in the required linked selections, and events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The room shown is the room tied to each qualifying census bed event for a patient. It is taken from census events that occurred during the chosen date range, using the data as it stood on the As Of date, excluding canceled events unless they were still active at that point, events not linked to a patient, events outside the two required linked record sets, and events in these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The bed name for each census event, based on the bed tied to that event.  
Time window: includes events that were supposed to happen during the chosen start and end dates, with the end date counted through the end of that day, and shows the data as it stood on the chosen As Of date; canceled events are still included if they were not deleted until after that As Of date.
[gate_passed] field: The patient service is the hospital service assigned to the patient for each census ADT event, such as the clinical service line responsible for the stay at that point in time. It is shown for census events that happened during the chosen date range, using the data as it existed as of the chosen As Of date, excluding canceled events that were already canceled by then, records without a patient, events outside the linked patient selections, and events in these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: If this field is populated, it is the date and time when the census event was canceled in the system. Time window: the report includes census events that were supposed to happen during the chosen start and end dates, treats the end date as inclusive, shows the data as it stood by the chosen As Of date, and keeps canceled events only when they were still not deleted by that As Of date.

It excludes canceled events that had already been deleted before that As Of date, events not linked to a patient or to the required related records, and events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.

[gate_passed] field: The room identifier from the census bed event, shown only for events during the chosen date range using an inclusive end date, as the data stood on the As Of date. Excludes canceled events that were already canceled before that As Of date, events entered after that As Of date, records without a patient, records not meeting the separately defined linked selections, and events in these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.

Excludes events without a patient, events not in the separately defined patient-linked selections, and events from these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
