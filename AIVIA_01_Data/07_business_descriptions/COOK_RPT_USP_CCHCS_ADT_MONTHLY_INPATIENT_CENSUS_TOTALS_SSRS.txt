==== COOK_RPT_USP_CCHCS_ADT_MONTHLY_INPATIENT_CENSUS_TOTALS_SSRS ====
basis 07.2.0

[gate_passed] file: One row is: one census ADT event for a patient, representing that patient’s census placement record for a specific event time.

Who's in it: patients with a recorded patient ID who have census ADT events dated from the start date through the end date, provided the event belonged to departments that passed the report’s service area and location selections, and provided the event was already on file as of the as-of date.

Each row shows: the event status, when it occurred, who the patient was, where the patient was assigned, and the related patient class and service context.

Time window: the report includes events whose event time falls on or after the start date and through the end date inclusive. It is also built as a snapshot of the data as it stood on the as-of date: the event must have been created before the day after that date. Events marked canceled are still kept if they were not deleted until after the as-of date, meaning they still existed in the record as of that date.

Excludes: events with no patient ID; events tied to departments outside the chosen service area or location selections, where choosing 0 means all values are accepted; and events from these units: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection contains census-style ADT event records for patients with a recorded patient ID, limited to events that were scheduled to occur during the requested date range, using an inclusive end date. It shows the event as it existed as of the reporting date: records had to be created before the day after that reporting date, and canceled records are still included only if they were not deleted until that day or later, so the dataset reflects what was on file at that point in time.

For each included census event, it adds the patient class, patient service, department, room, bed, and service area, along with the event time, census day, creation time, deletion time, and the event’s unique identifiers. It excludes events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR, and it only keeps records that also match two separately defined required selections.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection builds the allowed service area choices for the report by looking at the service area tied to each department and comparing it with the service area list the user picked. It keeps a row when the user selected that specific service area, or when the user included the special value zero, which acts as an all service areas choice. Each kept row simply carries a constant marker of one, so this step is just identifying which service areas are in scope.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection builds the allowed location list for the report. It keeps each chosen revenue location once, and it also allows the special choice value of 0, which means all locations rather than a specific one.

The available locations are derived from the department linked to the ADT event, using the department’s revenue location and related facility structure from the hospital service area setup. The supporting hospital bed, room, patient class, and patient service reference sources indicate this location choice is being applied in the context of inpatient or hospital ADT activity.
[gate_passed] field: This field labels the kind of ADT event, and in this extract it will always be Census because the report only keeps census events. In business terms, it tells you these rows are bed census location snapshots rather than other ADT activity such as admissions, transfers, or discharges.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows whether the census event was left active or later canceled, based on the event’s status in the ADT record. Because the report is built as of the chosen as-of date, an event canceled after that date is still treated as present in the historical view, while an event canceled by that date is not.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the date and time when the census event was effective for the patient location record. In business terms, it is the moment the patient was considered to be in that bed, room, and department, limited to events from the requested start date through the requested end date, with the end date treated as inclusive.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the patient identifier from the census event, so it tells you which patient the room or bed census record belongs to. It is only populated for events that had a patient recorded, were in the reporting date range, existed as of the selected as-of date, and were not in the excluded departments.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the hospital patient class in effect for the census event, using the standard abbreviated label from the patient class list. In business terms, it identifies the encounter type for that event, such as whether the patient was considered inpatient or another hospital class at that point in time.
[gate_passed] field: The field shows the abbreviated name of the department or unit tied to the patient’s census event, based on where the patient was assigned at the event’s effective time. It is drawn only for census events within the requested date range, as the data stood as of the as-of date, and excludes the listed procedural and specialty areas such as the emergency department, imaging, cath lab, operating rooms, psychiatry program, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: The report returns the room name for each census ADT event that falls within the selected date range, using an inclusive end date and reflecting the event exactly as it existed as of the As Of date. It excludes listed procedural and emergency departments, includes only records tied to a patient and the required related selections, and keeps canceled events only when they were still present as of that As Of date.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the name of the bed tied to the patient census event, using the bed assignment in effect for that event. It tells the business which specific bed the patient was assigned to for included census activity, based on records as they existed up to the as-of date.
[gate_passed] field: The patient service for the census event, shown as the standard service name attached to the patient’s hospital encounter at that time. In business terms, this is the clinical service responsible for the patient during that census event.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows when the census event record was actually removed from the system. In this report, that matters for reproducing the data as it stood on the as-of date: canceled census events are still included if they were not deleted until after that date.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the date and time when the census event record was first entered into the system. Because the report keeps records that were entered before the day after the as-of date, it reflects the event as it existed as of that reporting date.
[gate_passed] field: The service area shown is the service area attached to the department for that census event, based on where the patient was assigned at the event’s effective time. It reflects the event as it stood on the as-of date, including records later canceled only if they were still present as of that date.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the department’s internal unique identifier for the patient’s census event location, based on the unit attached to the event at the time it took effect. It comes only from census events for patients with a recorded patient ID that fall within the selected event date range, reflect the data as it existed as of the chosen as-of date, and exclude the listed departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the unique identifier for the room tied to each included census event, based on the room in effect for that event. The events are counted as they stood on the as-of date, within the selected date range using an inclusive end date, excluding the listed departments and including canceled events only if they were still present as of that date.
[gate_passed] field: The bed record ID is the unique identifier of the bed tied to each census event, based on the bed assignment attached to that event at the time it occurred. It is returned only for in-scope census activity for patients with a recorded patient ID, within the selected event date range, as the data stood as of the as-of date, and excluding the listed departments.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the unique identifier for each census event that was in scope for the report as it existed on the as-of date: the event had to have occurred from the start date through the end date inclusive, belong to a patient with a recorded patient ID, match two other required report selections, and be tied to a department other than CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, or DSC OR. Canceled events are still included if they were not deleted until after the as-of date, so this identifier represents the exact census event record counted under those timing and departmental rules.
[gate_passed] field: The calendar day taken from when the census event was scheduled to occur. In other words, this is the event’s census date with the time removed.
