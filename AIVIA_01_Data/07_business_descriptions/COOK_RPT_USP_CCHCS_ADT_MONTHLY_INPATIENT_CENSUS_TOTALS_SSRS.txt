==== COOK_RPT_USP_CCHCS_ADT_MONTHLY_INPATIENT_CENSUS_TOTALS_SSRS ====
basis 07.2.0

[gate_passed] file: One row is: one inpatient census event for one patient in one bed or room location, representing that patient being counted on a census day.

Who's in it: Patients with census events whose effective time falls during the requested reporting period, using the patient’s department, room, bed, patient class, patient service, and service area details linked to that event. When running the report, colleagues choose the service areas and locations to include; choosing All includes every service area or every location.

Each row shows: the patient’s census occurrence, where they were located, the clinical stay grouping, the organizational area, and identifying details for the event and location records.

Time window: The report includes census events from the start date through the end date, with the end date counted inclusively. It shows the data as it stood at the as-of date: only events entered by the end of that day are included, and canceled census events are still included if they were not deleted until after that day.

Excludes: Records not linked to a patient; departments CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection contains inpatient census ADT events for patients during the chosen date range, using the event date up to and including the end date, and showing the event as it existed as of the chosen as-of date. It includes the patient, patient class, service, unit, room, bed, service area, event day, and the event’s create and delete timestamps, while keeping canceled events only if they were still present as of that as-of date.

It excludes events from these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR. It also excludes records without a patient and any records that do not link to the required related selections.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the list of service areas chosen when running the report, based on the service area attached to each department. Choosing **All** means every service area is included; otherwise it includes only the specifically chosen service areas.

It is built from ADT event history and the department, room, bed, service area, and patient class reference records, so in practice it is identifying which service areas are represented by the hospital location tied to the ADT data. Each selected service area is carried forward as a simple included choice for the rest of the dataset build.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the set of locations chosen when running the report: either **All** by choosing **0**, or the specific **revenue location linked to a department**. It is built from ADT history and the linked bed, room, department, service area, patient class, and patient service reference records so the report can limit results to the relevant hospital location choices.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the type of event recorded for the row; in this report it will be the Census event type, meaning the bed census movement or status event that counts the patient in the inpatient census.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the subtype recorded on the census event, so it tells you what kind of census movement or status change the event represents within the broader census event category. In practice, this is the business-facing label that distinguishes one kind of census event from another, such as separating routine activity from a cancellation when that cancellation is still considered part of the reported history.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the date and time when the census event took effect for the patient, meaning the moment the bed status or patient placement change became active in operational terms. For business users, this is the clinically meaningful timestamp to use when you want to know when the patient’s census status actually changed, rather than when someone entered the event.
[gate_passed] field: This is the unique identifier for the patient linked to that census event, so it tells you which patient the bed or census record belongs to. It is the patient’s system ID rather than a display name, and it is used to reliably tie this row to the same patient across the record.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the abbreviated patient class for the census event, meaning the short label for the patient’s level or setting of care at that point in time, such as whether the patient was inpatient, observation, or in another standard class used by the hospital. It is the concise class code clinicians and operations staff use to quickly understand how the patient was being categorized for bed management, throughput, and reporting.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the abbreviated name of the department tied to the census event, so it tells you the clinical unit or area recorded for that bed status movement in a short department label rather than the full department name. For business users, this is the shorthand department identifier you would group or filter on when looking at where census activity occurred.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the name of the room tied to the patient’s census bed movement, so it tells you the room the patient was assigned to at that point in their stay. For a business reader, this is the room label staff would recognize on the unit, used to identify where the patient was physically located.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the label of the bed tied to the census event for that patient encounter, in other words the bed name staff would recognize on the unit. It tells business users which specific bed the patient was assigned to at that point in the stay.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the patient service name for the census event, meaning the clinical service or care team the patient was assigned under at that point in their stay, such as the service responsible for the patient’s inpatient care. It is the service label business users would recognize when looking at who clinically “owned” the patient during that census moment.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows when that census event was deleted from the record. For business users, it tells you whether the event was later removed and, if so, the exact date and time that happened.
[gate_passed] field: This is the timestamp when the census event was entered in the record. For business use, it tells you when that bed status or patient movement update was recorded, which may be different from when the change actually took effect.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the name of the service area tied to the census event, so it tells you which larger operational area of the organization the patient’s bed status belonged to, such as the broad service area that sits above the specific hospital location or department.
[gate_passed] field: This field gives the identifier of the department tied to the census event, so it tells you which department the patient was assigned to for that bed status record. In business terms, it is the department’s stored ID value, used to group or match activity by department rather than showing the department name.
[gate_passed] field: This field is the identifier for the room tied to the patient’s census bed stay event, so it tells you which room the patient was assigned to at that point in their stay. For business use, read it as the room reference from the bed management record rather than a narrative room name or description.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the identifier of the bed tied to that census event, in other words the specific bed the patient was assigned to at that point in their stay. For business users, it is the bed-level value you would use to tell exactly which bed the patient occupied, rather than just the room, unit, or department.
[gate_passed] field: This is the unique identifier for the census event itself, so it distinguishes one bed status or patient movement event from another in the source record set. It is mainly useful as a reference key when you need to trace a specific census event back to the underlying event record.
[gate_passed] field: This field gives the calendar day for the census event’s effective time, so it shows which census day that event belongs to in business terms. Use it as the day-level date for counting or grouping census activity, rather than the exact timestamp when the event took effect.
