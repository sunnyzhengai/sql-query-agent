==== COOK_RPT_USP_CCHCS_ADT_MONTHLY_INPATIENT_CENSUS_TOTALS_SSRS ====
basis 07.2.0

[gate_passed] file: One row is: one inpatient census event for one patient at one bed or room location, representing that patient’s census status at a specific effective date and time.

Who's in it: patients with a recorded Census event during the chosen reporting period, linked to a patient, in the chosen service area and chosen location. Choosing All for service area includes every service area, and choosing All for location includes every location.

Each row shows: where the patient was assigned, how the encounter was classified, the patient’s service, which service area and department the event belongs to, and identifiers and timing details for the census event.

Time window: the report includes census events with an effective date from the chosen start date through the chosen end date, including the full end date. It shows the data as it stood at the end of the chosen as-of date: only events entered by then are included. Events marked Canceled are still included if they were not deleted until after the as-of date, so the report reflects what users would have seen on that date. It also includes the day of month taken from the event’s effective date as the census day.

Excludes: records not linked to a patient; departments CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection contains census ADT events for patients with a recorded patient identifier, during the chosen event date range, using the data as it existed on the As Of date. It keeps events that were created before the end of the As Of date and excludes events canceled before that point, while still keeping events that were canceled later so the report reflects the historical state as of that date.

For each included census event, it brings back the patient, event date and census day, patient class, patient service, department, room, bed, service area, and the related department, room, bed, and ADT event identifiers. It excludes records for these departments: CCMC EMERGENCY, CCMC IR IMAGING, CCMC CATH LAB, CCMC MAIN OR, CCMC SPA OR, CCMC PHP PSYCHIATRY, CCMC CARDIOVASCULAR OR, and DSC OR, and also excludes records not linked to a patient or not matching the other required linked selections.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the report’s service area filter list. It includes one row for each department’s service area, and it also includes the special choice **All**, represented here by the value **0**.

In business terms, this lets the person running the report choose one or more service areas to limit the report, or choose **All** to include every service area. The list is built from hospital location and ADT reference information tied to departments, rooms, beds, service areas, patient class, and patient service reference data.
[gate_passed] scope: This selection is the list of revenue locations chosen when running the report. It includes either the specific revenue location linked to a department, or All when the choice is 0.

The location list is built from hospital event history together with bed, room, department, service area, and patient class information. Each row simply represents one chosen revenue location value for use in the report.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the type of event recorded for the row; in this report it will be the Census event type, which is the ADT-style census activity used to track a patient’s inpatient or hospital census status.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the subtype recorded on the census event, which tells the business meaning of that census update, such as whether it reflects a cancellation or another kind of census activity. It is the event’s more specific classification within the overall Census event category.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the date and time when the census event took effect for the patient encounter. For business users, this is the operational timestamp to use when understanding when the patient’s census status actually became active, which may differ from when the event was entered into the record.
[gate_passed] field: This field contains the identifier for the patient tied to the census event, so each value points to the specific person the row is about. It is the patient’s recorded ID from the medical record, used to distinguish one patient from another.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the abbreviated patient class recorded for the census event, such as the short form used in the medical record to indicate the patient’s care setting or registration status. For business users, it is the compact category that tells you how the patient was classified at that point in care.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the abbreviated department name for the department tied to the census event, so business users can see the short department label rather than the full department name. It identifies the operational unit associated with that census record.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the name of the room tied to the census event for the patient encounter, in other words the specific room where the patient was assigned at that point in their stay. For business users, it is the room label used to identify the patient’s physical placement within the unit or department.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the label of the bed tied to the census event, which is the bed name or identifier a business user would recognize on a unit or rooming board. It tells you which specific bed the patient was assigned to for that event.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the patient’s service for the census event, meaning the clinical or operational service the patient was assigned to at that point in care, such as the service line responsible for the patient’s stay. For business users, it identifies which hospital service the patient belonged to for that event.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows when the census event was deleted from the record. For business users, it is the date and time the event was removed; if it is blank, the event had not been deleted.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the date and time when the census event was recorded for the patient encounter. For business users, it is the timestamp of that census status event in the medical record.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the name of the service area tied to the census event, such as the broader operational area or facility grouping the patient encounter belongs to. For business users, it identifies which service area each returned record is associated with so results can be grouped, filtered, or compared at that organizational level.
[gate_passed] field: This field contains the identifier of the department tied to the census event record. For business users, it is the department ID for the unit or clinical area associated with that event, excluding the specific departments named in the report rules.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the identifier of the room tied to the census event, meaning the room where the patient was assigned or located for that recorded census activity. For business users, it is the room ID value used by the system to distinguish one room from another.
[gate_passed] field: This field shows the identifier of the bed tied to the patient’s census event, meaning the specific bed assignment associated with that stay activity. For business users, it is the bed ID for where the patient was assigned at that point in their encounter.
[gate_passed] field: This field holds the unique identifier for the census event record itself, so each value points to one specific census event in the source system. For a business reader, it is mainly a technical tracking number used to distinguish one event record from another and to link back to the exact event if needed.
[gate_passed] field: This field gives the calendar day taken from the event’s effective timestamp, so it shows which census day the event belongs to for reporting purposes. In business terms, it is the date you would use to place the census event on a daily census timeline.
